## 2. Organisations

Where each one holds territory is in [Factions](galaxy.md#3-factions) in galaxy.md.

| Organisation | Name | Description |
|--------------|------|-------------|
| Her company | Halden-Mori | Her former employer: a mercenary army and weapons maker, grown rich on the wars, and the biggest power in the Honeycomb. A merger of two firms, named after their founders: Ansel Halden, a mercenary general, and Sayo Mori, a weapons engineer, so the army and the arsenal share one name. Its bioweapons division, on a space station coreward of the cluster, builds weapons for contracts; this virus was off the books, and the company never knew of it or approved it. The encrypted drives are only found on its ships, which are medium risk |
| The cheap rival | Massey Contract Forces, "the Massey" | A mercenary company that fights with masses of cheap drones and conscripts. Founded by Bram Massey, a logistics contractor who worked out that drones and conscripts cost less than soldiers |
| The elite rival | Corvid | A small, expensive, high-tech mercenary company, hired as specialists: clever, few and watchful, like the crows it is named after. Founded by Isra Vell, a special-forces officer who left the core's army with her whole unit |
| The trader guild | The Hanse | Neutral; runs every trader hub in the cluster. Named after the medieval Hanseatic League, which ran ports across warring kingdoms and answered to none of them |
| Halden-Mori's patron | The Concord | An alliance of the core's trading houses; never seen, only mentioned |
| Massey's and Corvid's patron | The Sol Directorate | The government of the old worlds around Sol; never seen, only mentioned |

## 3. Historic figures

The people that places in the cluster are named after (see [History](galaxy.md#6-history) and
[Star systems](galaxy.md#8-star-systems) in galaxy.md).

| Who | Era | What they did |
|-----|-----|---------------|
| Captain Hollis Brandt | The survey | Commanded the survey ship *Kestrel Reach*, whose initials give the catalogue codes (KR-114) |
| Ezra and Ruth Calloway | The settling | Led the first landing, from the colony ship *Providence*, in the middle of the cluster, and named it Hearth: the settlers' capital |
| Maren Ostby | The settling | Led the second wave of settlers north from Hearth and founded the free north |
| "Old" Tobias Wren | The first company war | Held Holdfast against Halden-Mori for 200 days; its world was renamed Wren in his honour, and the battlefield there is Wren's Wall |
| Marshal Livia Dray | The first company war | The Halden-Mori commander who took Hearth and renamed it Praetor |
| Colonel Oren Pike | The long war | Led the Massey drone swarm that broke against Halden-Mori's lines at Goldenrod: the Long Fall, the biggest battlefield on the rim |
| Captain Sera Quill | The long war | Corvid's commander, who has held the crossings at Bluebell and Foxglove against every assault; the battlefield at Foxglove is Quill's Stand |
| Ansel Halden, Sayo Mori, Bram Massey, Isra Vell | Before the war | The companies' founders (see [Organisations](#2-organisations)) |
